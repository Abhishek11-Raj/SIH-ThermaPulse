STEP 3: PHYSIOLOGICAL THERMAL STRESS & EXPOSURE MEMORY [STATUS: COMPLETE]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Evidence & Findings:
- Thermal Indices Implemented:
  1. Heat Index: Rothfusz multivariate polynomial with Steadman low-heat fallback and dry/humid adjustments (app/thermal/heat_index.py).
  2. Wet-Bulb Temperature: Stull's empirical equation based on air temperature and relative humidity (app/thermal/wet_bulb.py).
  3. WBGT: Simplified Liljegren / Bernard model incorporating air temperature, humidity, solar radiation, and wind speed (app/thermal/wbgt.py).
  4. UTCI: 6th-order polynomial approximation of the Fiala multi-node human thermoregulation model (app/thermal/utci.py).
  5. Mean Radiant Temperature (MRT): Radiative balance calculation from solar flux, sky emissivity, and wind velocity (app/thermal/mrt.py).
- Nighttime Heat & Recovery:
  - Tmin calculation, hot-night threshold detection (>25°C), consecutive hot night tracking, and physiological recovery deficit modeling (app/thermal/nighttime.py).
- Heat Exposure Memory:
  - Stateful differential equation: E_t = λ * E_(t-1) + S_t - R_t (app/thermal/exposure_memory.py).
  - λ (persistence decay factor, default 0.95), S_t (current thermal stress contribution), R_t (nighttime recovery).
  - Persistence in exposure_memory_states table.
- Quality & Uncertainty Propagation: Quality flags and uncertainty bounds propagated from raw input data into thermal calculation outputs.
- Test Coverage: 31 tests passing in test_thermal.py.
