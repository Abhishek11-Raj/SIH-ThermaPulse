1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & ARCHITECTURAL FOUNDATIONS
===============================================================================
Step 8 closes the loop on the KESHAV heatwave early warning system. By connecting
real-world observed health outcomes with prior forecasts and issued alerts, Step 8
provides continuous empirical skill scoring, operational delivery evaluation,
data & concept drift monitoring, and controlled, auditable, versioned model governance.

Key Tenets Implemented:
1. STRICT PRIVACY: Aggregated ward/day health statistics only. Explicit rejection of PII.
2. MISSING DATA CLARITY: Absence of health outcome records is never treated as 0 events.
   Explicitly designated as NO_OUTCOME_AVAILABLE / UNVERIFIED.
3. ETHICAL TRANSPARENCY: All demo/synthetic test data explicitly flagged as `is_synthetic=True`
   with visible `SYNTHETIC / DEMO DATA` labels in API and UI.
4. CONTROLLED LEARNING: Autonomous self-replacement of production models is explicitly prohibited.
   All candidate promotions require temporal leakage validation, safety gate clearance,
   and explicit authorized human approval (Health Official / Clinical Director / Admin).
5. ROLLBACK CAPABILITY: Instant champion rollback to previous verified models with complete audit trail.

2. DATABASE ARCHITECTURE (SQLAlchemy Models)
===============================================================================
File: backend/app/models/learning.py (Exported in backend/app/models/__init__.py)

1. outcome_records (OutcomeRecordDB):
   - Ingests aggregated ward-level health events (heat stroke, dehydration, CVD, respiratory, ambulance calls).
   - Tracks data completeness, reporting delay hours, source type, and synthetic flag.
   - Version-tracked with revision pointers.

2. outcome_verifications (OutcomeVerificationDB):
   - Matches forecasts/predictions against observed outcome windows (24H, 48H, 72H, HORIZON).
   - Computes absolute/relative error, excess risk ratios, and contingency classifications
     (TRUE_POSITIVE, FALSE_POSITIVE, TRUE_NEGATIVE, FALSE_NEGATIVE, UNVERIFIED).

3. outcome_revisions (OutcomeRevisionDB):
   - Immutable audit trail for all retrospective corrections/revisions to health statistics.

4. alert_performance_records (AlertPerformanceDB):
   - Tracks delivery latency, timeliness (EARLY, ON_TIME, LATE, MISSED), provider channel status
     (separating live from mocked channels), acknowledgement status, and action feedback.

5. drift_records (DriftRecordDB):
   - Logs feature PSI (Population Stability Index), concept drift, calibration shift,
     OOD trend distributions, and protected subgroup equity disparities.

6. model_candidates (ModelCandidateDB):
   - Stores candidate model definitions, temporal cross-validation metrics, safety gate results,
     and human approval signatures.

7. model_deployments (ModelDeploymentDB):
   - Immutable log of champion model promotions, deployers, timestamps, and active status.

8. model_rollbacks (ModelRollbackDB):
   - Comprehensive audit log for champion model rollbacks with justification notes.

Database Schema Migration:
- Successfully initialized via init_db() across SQLite and PostgreSQL backends.
- Idempotent table creation verified across multiple test cycles.

3. CORE ENGINES & STATISTICAL ALGORITHMS
===============================================================================

1. OutcomeVerificationEngine (backend/app/learning/outcomes.py):
   - Temporal window matching with configurable tolerance (24H, 48H, 72H, HORIZON).
   - Baseline excess risk ratio computation relative to historical seasonal averages.
   - Robust missing-outcome handling with NO_OUTCOME_AVAILABLE / UNVERIFIED flags.
   - Immutable outcome revisions maintaining complete lineage.

2. ForecastTrackRecordEngine (backend/app/learning/track_record.py):
   - Continuous Skill Metrics: Mean Absolute Error (MAE), Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE).
   - Probabilistic Skill Metrics: Brier Score, Expected Calibration Error (ECE via equal-frequency binning).
   - Categorical / Contingency Skill Metrics: Precision (PPV), Probability of Detection (POD / Recall),
     False Alarm Ratio (FAR), Critical Success Index (CSI / Threat Score).
   - Discriminative Metrics: ROC-AUC, PR-AUC (with NumPy 1.x & 2.x trapezoidal compatibility).
   - Lead Time Analysis: Quantifies advance warning lead time across horizons.

3. AlertEffectivenessEngine (backend/app/learning/alert_effectiveness.py):
   - Operational Performance: Delivery success rates, channel latency, provider reliability.
   - Timeliness Distribution: Categorization into EARLY, ON_TIME, LATE, and MISSED.
   - Alert Fatigue & Response: Fatigue rate, suppressed alert counts, acknowledgement rate.
   - Mock Channel Segregation: Clear distinction between simulated providers and live gateways.

4. ModelHealthMonitor (backend/app/learning/drift_monitor.py):
   - Population Stability Index (PSI) algorithm for tabular feature drift detection (<0.10 NO_DRIFT,
     0.10-0.25 MODERATE_DRIFT, >0.25 SIGNIFICANT_DRIFT).
   - Concept Drift Detection: Flags widening calibration error or rising residual error.
   - Out-of-Distribution (OOD) Tracking: Aggregates Mahalanobis/IsolationForest anomaly rates.
   - Subgroup Equity Disparity: Compares False Discovery Rates (FDR) across vulnerable cohorts
     (e.g., outdoor workers, elderly, informal settlements) to detect algorithmic bias emergence.

5. ModelGovernanceEngine (backend/app/learning/governance.py):
   - Temporal Validation: Strictly enforces split dates to prevent future-data leakage.
   - Safety Gates:
     * Accuracy Gate (Brier score & ROC-AUC must meet minimum standards).
     * Calibration Gate (ECE must not exceed degradation thresholds).
     * Equity Gate (Subgroup disparity ratio must not exceed 1.25x).
     * Uncertainty Gate (Confidence intervals and epistemic uncertainty calibration).
   - Champion vs Challenger Comparison: Head-to-head scorecard across all validation metrics.
   - Human Approval Gate: Explicit sign-off required (ADMIN / HEALTH_OFFICIAL).
   - Production Promotion & Rollback: Audited activation of new champion models and emergency reversion.
