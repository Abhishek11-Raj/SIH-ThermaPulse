2. SUPPORTED INTERVENTIONS CATALOG & CONSTRAINTS
================================================================================

All eight (8) required intervention types are defined, registered, and validated:

1. OUTDOOR_EXPOSURE_REDUCTION (`outdoor_exposure_hours`)
   - Constraints: [0.0, 24.0] hours, direction: decrease
   - Modifies: Effective outdoor shift length, cumulative 24h/72h/168h exposure load
   - Domain: Thermal Stress & Exposure

2. WORK_REST_SCHEDULE (`rest_cycle_minutes_per_hour`)
   - Constraints: [0.0, 60.0] min/hr, direction: increase
   - Modifies: Effective metabolic rate factor (M_eff), lowering internal heat generation
   - Domain: Occupational Ergonomics & Thermal Stress

3. SHADE_INCREASE (`shade_fraction`)
   - Constraints: [0.0, 1.0] fraction, direction: increase
   - Modifies: Mean Radiant Temperature (MRT), solar radiative load, WBGT solar component
   - Domain: Microclimatic Radiative Shielding

4. WATER_ACCESS_INCREASE (`water_access_fraction`)
   - Constraints: [0.0, 1.0] fraction, direction: increase
   - Modifies: Physiological hydration vulnerability multiplier (mitigates dehydration risk)
   - Domain: Hydration & Vulnerability

5. COOLING_ACCESS_INCREASE (`cooling_access_fraction`)
   - Constraints: [0.0, 1.0] fraction, direction: increase
   - Modifies: Diurnal/nighttime thermal recovery coefficient, reduces exposure memory
   - Domain: Physiological Recovery & Exposure Memory

6. POWER_RESTORATION (`power_reliability_fraction`)
   - Constraints: [0.0, 1.0] fraction, direction: increase
   - Modifies: Cooling availability ceiling and operational infrastructure resilience
   - Domain: Grid Infrastructure & Vulnerability

7. EXPOSURE_TIME_SHIFT (`exposure_hour_shift`)
   - Constraints: [-6.0, 6.0] hours, direction: either
   - Modifies: Diurnal thermal index alignment (shifting exposure to cooler morning/evening hours)
   - Note: Strictly preserves ambient meteorological baseline measurements
   - Domain: Temporal Exposure Scheduling

8. COMBINED (`combined_package`)
   - Constraints: [0.0, 1.0] package intensity, direction: increase
   - Modifies: Co-optimized combination of shade, rest cycles, hydration, and cooling
   - Domain: Integrated Public Health Policy
