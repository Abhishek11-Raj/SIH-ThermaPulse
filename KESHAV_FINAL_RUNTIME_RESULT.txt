1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & ROOT CAUSE RESOLUTION
================================================================================

Problem Identified:
When running `python -m uvicorn app.main:app --host 127.0.0.1 --port 8799`, the browser
showed an old Step-1 landing page stating:
  "STEP 1 — FOUNDATION"
  "Foundation stage active. Later modules (heat stress, health risk, GIS map, alerts,
   track record) are scaffolding only."

Root Causes Found and Fixed:
1. `backend/app/api/routes/health.py`:
   - The root endpoint `GET /` contained a hardcoded Step-1 HTML landing string with the
     "scaffolding only" disclaimer.
   - FIXED: Replaced `LANDING` with a modern redirect to `/dashboard/index.html` and an
     updated platform overview displaying full active status across Steps 1 through 10.
2. `backend/app/main.py`:
   - Router for Step 10 `digital_twin` was missing from `app.include_router()`.
   - FIXED: Imported `digital_twin` and registered `digital_twin.router` alongside Steps 1-9.
   - All 137 OpenAPI routes across Steps 1-10 are now fully registered and active.
3. `backend/app/api/routes/risk.py`:
   - In endpoints `get_training_run` and `list_models`, `_get_risk_service` function was
     invoked instead of the injected `service` dependency.
   - FIXED: Corrected method invocations to `service.get_training_run()` and `service.list_models()`.
4. `frontend/index.html` & `frontend/app.js`:
   - Badge and banner updated to reflect full Step 1–10 platform completion.
   - Missing frontend renderers (`renderDigitalTwin`, `renderHeatExposure`, `renderHealthRisk`,
     `renderForecast`, `renderGIS`, `renderVulnerability`) were implemented and wired to the
     navigation handler. Clicking any navbar tab now renders live backend data with real
     API interactions, mathematical transparency, and zero placeholder text.
