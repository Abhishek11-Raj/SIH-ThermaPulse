2. NAVIGATION STRUCTURE
================================================================================

A. Public Navigation (Default Citizen View — 6 Tabs):
   - 🏠 Home: Instant risk assessment, 5-day glance, plain-language drivers, and action steps.
   - 🌡 Heat & Weather: Air temp, feels-like, WBGT, humidity, night recovery, and solar radiation.
   - 📅 5-Day Forecast: Multi-day temperature, humidity, risk tiers, and daily recommendations.
   - 🗺 Heat Map: Ward-level spatial risk cards with 1-click ward inspection.
   - ❤️ Health & Safety: Cohort safety (workers, elderly, children) and symptom checker (heat exhaustion vs stroke).
   - ℹ About KESHAV: Mission, physical science foundations, and privacy ethics.

B. Authority Navigation (Steps 1–10 Management Suite):
   - 🏢 Operations Center: Ward priority rankings, supply-demand deficits, and municipal response plans.
   - 🌐 Digital Twin & Resilience: 10-D resilience index, SSP climate scenarios, Pareto frontier portfolios, and roadmap.
   - 🧪 What-If Simulation: Model-based counterfactual intervention experiments and equity checks.
   - 🚨 Alerts & Governance: Multi-channel dispatch (SMS, WhatsApp, sirens) and delivery logs.
   - 📈 Forecast Track Record: Brier score, ROC-AUC, ECE calibration, and outcome verification.
   - 🤖 Model Health & System: Feature PSI drift, concept drift, champion/challenger training, and provider registry.
   - 📊 Data Quality & Audits: Stream quality rollups and ingestion logs.
   - ← Return to Public View: Instant toggle back to citizen interface.
