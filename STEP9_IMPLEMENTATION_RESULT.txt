2. ARCHITECTURAL OVERVIEW & SCIENTIFIC BOUNDARIES
===============================================================================
Step 9 transforms KESHAV from a retrospective and predictive platform into an
actionable, closed-loop urban decision support system. It connects predicted risks
and active alerts with real-world municipal infrastructure, field teams, and
healthcare capacity.

Key Tenets Implemented:
1. DECISION SUPPORT ONLY: Optimization models and priority scores produce advisory
   recommendations. Real-world actions and government resource deployments are
   NEVER executed autonomously.
2. HUMAN AUTHORIZATION: Operational plans require explicit sign-off from authorized
   personnel (HEALTH_OFFICIAL, MUNICIPAL_OPERATOR, ADMIN).
3. NON-CAUSAL COUNTERFACTUALS: Counterfactual scenario evaluations maintain strict
   qualification as `MODEL_BASED_COUNTERFACTUAL`, never claiming unproven causal effects.
4. EQUITY ASSURANCE: Resource allocation enforces minimum protection floors for
   high-vulnerability wards and computes real-time disparity ratios.
5. COMPLETE PRIVACY: Operates strictly on ward/depot aggregate statistics. No patient
   or individual PII is stored.

3. OPERATIONAL DOMAIN ENTITIES & DATABASE SCHEMA
===============================================================================
File: backend/app/models/city_operations.py (Exported in backend/app/models/__init__.py)

1. operational_zones (OperationalZoneDB):
   - Ward-level operational demographics, vulnerable population counts, outdoor worker counts,
     baseline cooling and water capacities, hospital beds, and priority scores.
2. resources (ResourceDB):
   - Physical and personnel resource inventory (COOLING_CENTER, DRINKING_WATER_POINT,
     HYDRATION_ORS_STATION, AMBULANCE, MEDICAL_FACILITY_BEDS, FIELD_WORKER_TEAM,
     MOBILE_COOLING_UNIT, WATER_TANKER, SHADED_REST_AREA).
   - Tracks location coordinates, capacity, available units, operating hours, and status.
3. resource_gaps (ResourceGapDB):
   - Quantified supply deficits versus modeled population demand across cooling, water, ORS,
     and medical surge dimensions.
4. operational_plans (OperationalPlanDB):
   - Coordinated municipal response plans with full lifecycle tracking:
     DRAFT -> REVIEW -> APPROVED / REJECTED -> EXECUTED -> CANCELLED.
   - Tracks SLA targets, forecast basis, and human approval notes.
5. action_tasks (ActionTaskDB):
   - Granular tasks decomposed from plans, assigned to specific teams (PLANNED, ASSIGNED,
     IN_PROGRESS, COMPLETED, FAILED) with evidence feedback notes.
6. response_executions (ResponseExecutionDB):
   - Audit records capturing reported vs verified coverage, response latencies, and unmet demand.
7. operational_scenarios (OperationalScenarioDB):
   - City-scale what-if scenario simulations combining resource shifts with Step 6 interventions.
8. operational_policies (OperationalPolicyDB):
   - Configurable, versioned decision policies with priority weights and SLA thresholds.

4. CORE OPERATIONAL ENGINES & ALGORITHMS
===============================================================================

1. OperationalPriorityEngine (backend/app/operations/priority.py):
   - Multi-criteria priority formula evaluating:
     * Health Risk Probability (Weight: 0.25)
     * Thermal Stress Severity (Weight: 0.15)
     * Nighttime Recovery Deficit (Weight: 0.10)
     * Vulnerable Population Fraction (Weight: 0.15)
     * Outdoor Worker Density (Weight: 0.10)
     * Protective Resource Shortage (Weight: 0.10)
     * Healthcare System Stress (Weight: 0.05)
     * Alert Severity Level (Weight: 0.05)
     * Forecast Lead Time Urgency (Weight: 0.05)
   - Applies uncertainty & OOD penalties when sensor telemetry or forecasts are unstable.
   - Produces itemized, transparent factor contributions and urgency recommendations.

2. ResourceGapEngine (backend/app/operations/resource_gap.py):
   - Models population demand for cooling, hydration (Liters/day), ORS packets, and hospital surge.
   - Evaluates spatial deficits, travel times, and coverage ratios.
   - Assigns severity ratings (LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH, CRITICAL).

3. ResourceAllocationEngine (backend/app/operations/allocation.py):
   - Priority-weighted optimization for mobile resources (tankers, mobile cooling, field teams,
     ORS packets, ambulances).
   - Solves supply constraints while enforcing minimum equity guarantees.
   - Performs Equity Audit measuring disparity ratio between vulnerable and general populations.

4. ScenarioOrchestrationEngine (backend/app/operations/scenario_orchestration.py):
   - Directly calls Step 6 `InterventionEngine` to compute model-based counterfactual risks.
   - Checks operational feasibility against municipal supply limits.
   - Compares multi-scenario matrices and recommends optimal feasible interventions.

5. ResponsePlanEngine (backend/app/operations/response_plan.py):
   - Decomposes high-level response directives into actionable field tasks.
   - Evaluates SLA compliance against target windows (WITHIN_SLA / BREACHED_SLA).
   - Computes operational completion rates and feeds into Step 8 closed-loop evaluation.

6. CityResilienceEngine (backend/app/operations/resilience.py):
   - Evaluates 6 city readiness dimensions:
     1. Forecast & Warning Lead Time
     2. Alert Governance & Dispatch
     3. Protective Resource Supply
     4. Healthcare & Emergency Bed Surge
     5. Field Worker & Community Outreach
     6. Telemetry & Sensor Freshness
   - Performs compound hazard analysis (Heat + Pollution) and cascading grid stress modeling.
